## Rationale

We allocated $600,000 equally across four trader seats, with each member independently responsible for developing, testing, and executing their own trading strategy. This structure allows us to explore different strategies in parallel and compare performance under equal starting capital. Separating the trader accounts also helps us evaluate individual strategies and manage risk.

The remaining $400,000 was allocated to one broker desk, jointly managed by all four members. The broker operates as a market maker, posting bid and ask quotes to earn spreads while managing inventory and adverse selection risk. Allocating 40% of our capital to the broker provides a larger buffer for inventory fluctuations and supports continuous two-sided quoting.

We chose not to operate an exchange so that our team could focus on trading strategy development, market making, execution quality, and risk management.
